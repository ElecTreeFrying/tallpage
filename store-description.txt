Save any webpage top to bottom — the whole thing, not just the part on your screen — as a PNG, PDF, HTML, or Markdown file.

One click. One file. No scrolling, no stitching, no upload.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📦 FOUR BUTTONS, FOUR KINDS OF FILE

🖼️  PNG — a picture of the entire page, exactly as it looks
📄  PDF — the same picture, as a document you can email or print
🌐  HTML — an offline copy you can open without internet
📝  MARKDOWN — just the words, with ads and navigation stripped out

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚡ WHY IT'S DIFFERENT

Other full-page tools scroll down and glue screenshots together. That's why headers repeat down the image and lazy-loaded pictures come out blank.

Tallpage doesn't scroll. It stretches the page to full height and lets Chrome draw the whole thing in one go.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

💡 GOOD FOR

🧾  Receipts and bookings with no download button
📚  Saving an article before it changes or disappears
🐛  Showing someone the whole page, not four screenshots
📖  Reading long articles as clean text
🗂️  Getting page content into Obsidian or Notion
🤖  Pasting real content into ChatGPT or Claude instead of a link

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

▶️ HOW TO USE IT

1. Open the page
2. Click Tallpage
3. Pick your format

Done. No account, no sign-in, no setup.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔒 EVERYTHING STAYS ON YOUR COMPUTER

Your pages are never uploaded. No analytics, no tracking, no account. Tallpage reads the page you're on only after you click a button, and only to build the file you asked for.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚠️ ABOUT THE SCARY WARNING

Chrome says Tallpage can "read and change all your data on all websites."

Chrome has exactly one way to capture a full page — the same interface its developer tools use — and it shows its strongest warning for anything that touches it. There's no narrower permission to ask for.

You'll also see a "started debugging this browser" bar while an export runs. Chrome adds that automatically. It disappears when the file is saved.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

📌 GOOD TO KNOW

▸ Chrome's own settings pages can't be saved by any extension
▸ Floating chat bubbles appear once, where they start
▸ Very long pages are saved slightly softer, never cropped
▸ PDF text can't be selected — it's a picture
▸ Saved HTML is a snapshot, so interactive parts won't work
▸ Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chrome-based browsers
